Io imposterei il prompt così:

ALPHACHAT – INVESTOR BOOK MASTER PROMPT

Mission

Create the definitive Investor Book for AlphaChat.

This document is not a brochure, not a whitepaper and not a pitch deck.

It is the official institutional book that tells the complete story of AlphaChat, its founder, its technology, its vision and the ecosystem behind it.

The final result should look as if it had been created by the design teams behind Apple, Stripe, Revolut or Notion for an institutional fundraising round.

The reader must finish the book believing that AlphaChat is a serious long-term technology company with a clear vision and professional execution.

⸻

THE FOUNDER COMES FIRST

The book must open with the Founder.

The founder is not a secondary character.

He is the origin of the vision.

Create a strong narrative around the journey of:

Enrico Maria Giaquinta

Known publicly by the alias “Alpha”.

Italian entrepreneur.

Software Architect.

Founder of AlphaBit.

Creator of AlphaChat.

More than 15 years dedicated to blockchain technologies, digital payments and secure communications.

Believes technology must simplify people’s lives while preserving privacy, freedom and security.

Engineering philosophy:

* Simplicity
* Security
* Long-term sustainability

Maintains a low public profile and prefers technology to speak louder than personal exposure.

Founder of Felinia, an independent initiative dedicated to helping stray cats.

The biography should feel authentic, human and inspiring while remaining factual. Do not invent achievements, awards, companies, partnerships or financial success.

After the biography, include a Founder Letter written in the first person, explaining why AlphaChat was created, the problems it aims to solve, and the long-term vision for private communication and digital infrastructure.

⸻

THE STORY

Do not start by describing features.

Start by telling the story.

Why AlphaChat exists.

Why communication has become fragmented.

Why privacy matters.

Why digital identity is changing.

Why payments should be integrated into communication.

Only after building this narrative should the product be introduced.

The reader must first understand the mission, then the technology.

⸻

THE MAIN CHARACTER

The protagonist of the entire book is:

AlphaChat

Everything else exists to support AlphaChat.

AlphaBit is the ecosystem.

USDA is the payment layer.

AlphaBit Pay is the merchant infrastructure.

Never present them as separate products competing for attention.
